Preface

Welcome to Foundations of World Models.

This book provides a comprehensive exploration of World Models—AI systems that build internal representations of their environment to understand, predict, and plan. Drawing from advances in deep learning, reinforcement learning, and cognitive science, World Models represent a paradigm shift from reactive AI systems to ones capable of imagination and foresight.

What Are World Models?

A World Model is an internal simulation that allows an AI agent to:

  • Understand the underlying dynamics of its environment
  • Predict future states based on potential actions
  • Plan optimal strategies without real-world trial-and-error

This capability—learning a compressed, predictive representation of the world—mirrors how biological intelligence navigates complex environments.

Who Is This Book For?

This book is designed for:

  • Researchers exploring the frontiers of embodied AI and predictive learning
  • Engineers building intelligent systems that require planning and reasoning
  • Students with a foundation in deep learning seeking to understand state-of-the-art architectures

A background in neural networks, basic reinforcement learning, and linear algebra is recommended.

How to Use This Book

The book is structured progressively, building from fundamental components to complete architectures:

Part Chapters Focus
Foundations 1-3 Self-attention, Transformers, and core mechanisms
Perception 4-5 Image encoders, generative models, and representation learning
Decision Making 6-7 MDPs, Bellman equations, and reinforcement learning
World Models 8-9 DINO-WM, JEPA, and predictive architectures

Each chapter includes mathematical foundations, architectural details, and connections to the broader World Models framework.

Acknowledgments

This work builds upon groundbreaking research from the AI community. Special acknowledgment goes to the researchers behind DINO, JEPA, and the many innovations that make World Models possible.

Let’s begin the journey of building machines that can imagine.
