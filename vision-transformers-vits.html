Image Encoders with DINO and DINOv2

Chapter 13 Vision Transformers (ViTs)

ViTs apply **self-attention** to image data, treating images as sequences of patches rather than using convolutional operations.

VISION TRANFORMER

13.1 Patch Extraction and Encoding

An image is split into patches, flattened, and passed through a **linear projection**: \[X_{patch} = W_E \cdot X_{input} + b_E\] where \(W_E\) is the embedding matrix and \(b_E\) is a bias term. These embeddings are then processed using self-attention layers to extract contextual information.
