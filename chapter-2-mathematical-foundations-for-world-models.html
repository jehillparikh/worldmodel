3.1 Probability & Information Theory

  • Bayesian Inference: Probabilistic reasoning in AI.
  • KL Divergence: Measures differences in probability distributions.
    \[ D_{KL}( P || Q ) = \sum_{x} P(x) \log \frac{P(x)}{Q(x)} \]
  • Shannon Entropy: Foundation of generative modeling.
